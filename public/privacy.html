Privacy Policy

How Equitas Law collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 1 January 2026

Equitas Law Advocates ("Equitas Law", "the Firm", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you entrust to us. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we handle it, and the rights you have under the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Kenya) and, where applicable, the General Data Protection Regulation (EU/UK).

1. Who we are

The data controller is Equitas Law, Equitas House, 5th Floor, Kenyatta Avenue, P.O. Box 21045-00100, Nairobi, Kenya. Questions about this policy or your data may be sent to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@equitaslaw.co.ke or on +254 711 000 222.

2. Personal data we collect

We only collect what we need to respond to you and to provide legal services. This may include:

  • Identity data — your name and the capacity in which you contact us.
  • Contact data — your email address, telephone number and postal address.
  • Matter data — the description of your legal issue and any documents you choose to share.
  • Technical data — limited information such as your IP address and browser type, collected automatically for security and rate-limiting purposes.

We ask that you do not send us highly sensitive information (for example, health records or financial account details) through the website form until we have confirmed that we can act for you and have put a secure channel in place.

3. How we use your data and our lawful basis

  • To respond to consultation requests and enquiries — on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in corresponding with you.
  • To provide, and administer, legal services — to perform our contract with you or to take steps at your request before entering into it.
  • To comply with statutory, professional and anti-money-laundering obligations — to meet our legal obligations.
  • To protect the security and integrity of our website — on the basis of our legitimate interests.

4. Legal professional privilege

Communications with us for the purpose of obtaining legal advice may be protected by legal professional privilege. We treat all client information as confidential, but privilege only attaches once a solicitor–client relationship has been established.

5. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only with:

  • Advocates, pupils and staff within the Firm who are working on your matter.
  • Courts, tribunals, regulators and opposing parties where the law or the conduct of your matter requires it.
  • Trusted service providers (for example, our hosted IT providers) who process data on our documented instructions and under confidentiality obligations.

6. Storage, transfers and retention

Your data is stored on servers located in the European Economic Area and Kenya. Where we transfer data outside Kenya we rely on appropriate safeguards permitted under the Act. We retain client files, matter data and website enquiries for as long as necessary to provide our services and to satisfy professional and statutory record-keeping requirements — generally seven years after a matter closes, and longer where a claim or legal obligation requires it.

7. Your rights

Subject to certain exceptions, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used, and to access a copy of it;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of data when there is no compelling reason for us to keep it;
  • object to, or request restriction of, processing based on legitimate interests;
  • request portability of data you provided to us, in a structured, machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing;
  • lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya) or your local supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@equitaslaw.co.ke. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and analytics

This website is built without advertising trackers or third-party analytics cookies. We use no cookies to identify you personally. Any storage used is strictly necessary to remember interface preferences such as whether you have dismissed the site notice.

9. Security

We apply administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit, access controls, and the principle of least privilege. No system is perfectly secure; if you suspect a data breach, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be highlighted on the website.

This policy is provided for transparency and does not create legal-advice obligations or a solicitor–client relationship. See our Terms of Use.
