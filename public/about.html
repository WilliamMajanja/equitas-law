A Chambers Built on a Single Word: Equitas

Fairness. Equity. Even-handed justice. The Roman ideal that names us is the standard we hold ourselves to on every brief, for every client, every day.

Our Story

From Two Desks on Kenyatta Avenue to a Full-Service Firm

Equitas Law was founded in 2011 by Diana A. Mwangi, then a young litigator with a borrowed office, two files and an unshakeable conviction: that Kenyan clients deserved counsel that was technically excellent, commercially aware and honest about cost.

Fifteen years later, the firm has grown into a chambers of eight advocates and a full professional staff — but the founding discipline has never changed. Every file is opened with a strategy memo, every fee is agreed in writing, and every client hears the unvarnished truth about their position.
