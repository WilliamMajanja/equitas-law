Common Questions

Before You Instruct Us

How are your fees structured? +
Every engagement begins with a written, itemised quotation. Depending on the matter we work on fixed fees, capped fees or the Advocates Remuneration Order scale — agreed with you before any work begins. There are no surprise invoices at Equitas.
Is the first consultation really confidential? +
Yes. Advocate–client privilege attaches from the first conversation, whether or not you ultimately instruct us. Nothing you share is disclosed to any third party, ever, without your written consent or a court order.
Do you act for clients outside Nairobi or outside Kenya? +
Regularly. We appear in courts and registries across all 47 counties, and a significant share of our conveyancing and succession work is for diaspora clients — conducted by video conference, secure document exchange and registered power of attorney where needed.
How quickly will my matter move? +
You receive a written timetable with your strategy memo. Court timelines are partly outside any firm's control, but we commit to same-day responses, proactive progress reports and immediate notification of every development in your file.
